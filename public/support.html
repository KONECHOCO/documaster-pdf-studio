Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to konechocoproduction@gmail.com. We answer in Italian, English, French and Spanish.

FAQ

Are my files uploaded? No. Every tool works on your device; see the privacy policy.

OCR says it cannot download the language model. The first OCR in each language needs an internet connection once (2-4 MB). After that it works offline.

How do I restore DocuMaster Pro on a new phone? Open Pro from the top bar and tap “Restore purchases”, using the same Apple ID or Google account.

I forgot the password of a PDF I protected. The password is never stored anywhere: without it the file cannot be opened. Keep a copy of the original file.
